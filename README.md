# 👻 HIT OR STAND 🎲

**Dead. Broke. Still betting.**

*A survival game inspired by Vampire Survivors.*

---

</div>

You’re a little ghost with a big gambling addiction. Death took your pulse, but it couldn’t take your terrible financial decisions.

The casino won’t fund itself. Head into the battlefield, slaughter the monsters, and turn your winnings into one more spin. Pick up spells, bring a pet, and try to survive long enough to pay for your next bad idea.

Back at the casino, you swear this is the last bet.

**It never is.**

<div align="center">

⚔️ **Kill.** → 🪙 **Collect.** → 🎰 **Gamble.** → 🔁 **Repeat.**
